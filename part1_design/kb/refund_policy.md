# Refund Policy
[R1] Monthly plans can be refunded within 14 days of the first payment if no more than 10 GB of traffic has been used.
[R2] Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, a pro-rated credit may be issued at the discretion of the billing team.
[R3] Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5-10 business days.
[R4] Add-ons (domain registration, SSL certificates) are non-refundable.
