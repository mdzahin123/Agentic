# FAQ

[F2] Invoices are available under Dashboard > Billing > Invoices and can be downloaded as PDF.
[F3] Feedback and feature requests can be submitted at the in-app feedback form; the product team reviews them weekly.
[F4] Supported browsers are the latest two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge.
