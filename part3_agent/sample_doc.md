# Nimbus Cloud Hosting - Plans & Policies (sample document)

## Plans (prices in USD, billed monthly unless stated)
- Starter: $5 per month, 1 website, 10 GB storage, 100 GB bandwidth.
- Pro: $15 per month, 10 websites, 100 GB storage, 1 TB bandwidth, daily backups.
- Business: $40 per month, unlimited websites, 500 GB storage, unlimited bandwidth, hourly backups, priority support.
- Annual billing gives a 20% discount on the monthly price (billed once per year).

## Refunds
- Monthly plans: refundable within 14 days of the first payment.
- Annual plans: refundable within 30 days of purchase.
- Domain registration and SSL certificates are non-refundable.

## Support
- Starter and Pro: email support, replies within 24 hours.
- Business: 24/7 live chat and phone, first response within 1 hour.
- Support hours for email: Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 (UTC+8).

## Security
- All plans include free SSL certificates and daily malware scanning.
- Two-factor authentication is available on every plan and recommended for admins.
